What's the difference between the Brother DCP-L2540DW and MFC-L2700D?

The DCP-L2540DW is a 3-in-1 laser printer (print, scan, copy) with WiFi, good for wireless printing from a laptop or phone. The MFC-L2700D is the 5-in-1 version that also includes fax — the right pick if your office still needs fax capability. Both are available at Levo Box in Makassar.

Can LC583 ink be used in a laser printer?

No. LC583 ink is designed for inkjet J-series printers (like the MFC-J2510, MFC-J3520). Laser printers like the DCP-L2540DW and MFC-L2700D use toner cartridges, not liquid ink — the two aren't interchangeable. Always check your printer type before buying ink or toner.

What's the difference between comb binding and wire binding?

Comb binding uses a reusable plastic comb that opens and closes, good if pages need to be added or swapped later. Wire binding uses a double-loop wire that looks neater and lasts longer, and can open flat for documents that get read often. Levo Box carries TATA binding machines for both formats, including 3-in-1 models that handle either.

What binding capacity is good for office use?

For small-to-medium office use, a machine with 150-200 sheet binding capacity is usually enough. For higher volume or thick reports, look for a machine rated up to 500 sheets. Levo Box carries several TATA binding machines from economical to heavy-duty tiers — message us on WhatsApp for a recommendation that fits your needs.

Is there a cheaper alternative to original Brother ink?

Yes. Alongside original Brother ink, compatible alternative ink and modified refillable cartridges are available at a more affordable price. Levo Box carries both options — original ink for factory-standard performance, or the alternative for a more budget-friendly choice.
